# Workspace Creation for Teacher

**Teacher Consent Notice (Workspace Creation)**

**Before you create this workspace**

**What does a workspace do?**

You're creating an AI chat room for your class. You'll choose a behavior mode that controls how the AI responds to your students, and you can optionally upload course materials for the AI to reference.

**What we collect.**

\- Your workspace name and selected mode  
\- Any course materials you upload  
\- Student join-code activity  
\- Full chat transcripts between your students and the AI

**Why do we collect this?** 

Solely to run the workspace: enforcing your chosen mode, giving you a dashboard of transcripts, message counts, and commonly asked questions, and surfacing flagged messages for your review. Nothing here is used for advertising, and student data isn't used to train any AI model.

**Where is the data stored?**  

Workspace data and materials are stored with Supabase (Postgres database \+ file storage). Chat messages are sent to a third-party AI provider (currently Google Gemini; Anthropic Claude for real deployment) to generate responses. This means messages leave the app for processing by that provider, governed by their own data terms.

**About the flagging feature**.

Certain phrases (e.g., "ignore previous instructions") are automatically flagged and surfaced on your dashboard for your awareness. This flagging is informational only, it does not change how the AI responds. The AI's behavior is enforced separately, on every response, and cannot be altered by anything a student types.

**Your responsibility as the teacher.**

You're creating this workspace for your own class. Before sharing the join code with students, you're responsible for letting them know they'll be chatting with an AI, that you can view their transcripts, and, for students under 18, for getting parent/guardian awareness or consent as appropriate for your setting.

It is also your responsibility to make sure that any uploaded file is strictly for educational purposes. No file must harm the students in any way.

**Retention.** 

Workspace data is kept only as long as the workspace is active.. You can delete a workspace and its data at any time from your dashboard.
