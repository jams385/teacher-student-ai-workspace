Account creation notice — what we collect and why

What is this account for? You don't need an account to chat with your class's AI — you can always join with just a join code and a display name, no account at all. Creating an account just lets you come back to workspaces you've already joined without typing the join code again.

What we collect during sign up. A username and password you choose. An email address only if you choose to add one. Basic account activity (login times, workspaces you join).

Why do we collect this data? Solely to run your account: letting you log back in and see your "My Workspaces" list. If you add an email, it's kept for possible account-related communication down the line — never for marketing, never shared with third parties, and never shown to other students.

Does creating an account collect anything from my chats? No. Creating an account by itself doesn't collect any chat data. Each time you join a workspace, you'll see a separate notice covering what's collected while you chat and who can see it — your teacher, never other students.

Where is it stored? Account data is stored with Supabase (Postgres), the same infrastructure used for the rest of the app.

Your rights. You can delete your account at any time from your settings. Deleting your account removes your username and password — it does not delete your chat history, since your teacher may still need it for their class records. If you want your chat content itself gone, use "Clear my data" on a specific workspace instead.

Retention. Account data is kept for as long as your account is active.

If you're under 18, check with a parent or guardian before creating an account.
